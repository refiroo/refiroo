---
title: Change values of many records
description: Set or clear fields on selected records after reviewing a preview.
reader: steward
---

Use this for the same field correction on several records. Read
[Changing many records](/docs/use/changing-many-records/) for selection, access and limits.

1. In an editable collection, tick the records or choose **Select all … matching**. Check the
   count and any exclusions in the selection bar, then choose **Change values**.
2. Choose **Change a field** and select a field. Choose **Set to** and enter its new value,
   or **Clear** for an optional field. Required fields cannot be cleared. Read-only and hidden
   fields are not offered. Repeat to change more fields together.
3. Leave a field out to keep its value. To remove a field you added, use its **Do not change …**
   button. This form does not need a Keep choice on every field.
4. Choose **Change the rest** to apply records that can change despite other refusals, or
   **Change none** to require every item to pass. If the second option is unavailable, the
   dialog explains the deployment's all-or-nothing limit.
5. Choose **Preview**. Read how many records would change, already hold the values, or would
   be refused. Open a refused record to understand its reason. Use **Back** to revise the change.
6. Choose **Apply**, then read the actual result. A record changed by someone else is refused.
   **Preview again** checks remaining work against current values. Choose **Done** when finished.

**View job** leaves a preview waiting on its job page. Closing the preview otherwise cancels
it. If the model changed, reload before making a fresh change; if the change set changed,
preview again. A successful preview never reserves a record against another editor.

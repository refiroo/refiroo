---
title: Set up email notices
description: Turn on email, choose notice kinds and check your digest schedule.
reader: steward
---

Choose email copies without changing your inbox. See
[Notification settings](/docs/use/notifications-settings/) for eligibility and limits.

1. Open **Settings › Notification email**, or choose **Notification email settings** from
   the Notifications page. Check the address under **Email**.
2. Turn on **Email me when something arrives in my inbox**. If no switch is offered, read the
   reason: mail may not be set up or your account may have no address. Ask an administrator
   to resolve it; following workspaces can still work without mail.
3. Under **How each kind reaches me**, choose **At once**, **Digest** or **Off** for each kind.
   Changes save immediately. If an error appears, correct the cause and try again.
4. For a digest, choose **Every morning at 07:00** or **Mondays at 07:00** under **The digest**.
   Read the displayed time zone and **Next digest**. The browser supplies the zone; the hour
   is fixed. The first kind put into Digest starts a daily schedule automatically.
5. Return to **Notifications** for the full inbox. Reading a notice before its digest is sent
   leaves it out. Nothing eligible means no digest; older unread notices are not excluded
   merely because they are more than a day old.

To stop all email, turn the master switch off. To stop just one kind, choose **Off** beside
it. Inbox notices remain either way. Immediate mail does not backfill notices from before
you enabled email and only sends unread notices less than a day old.

---
title: Delete many records
description: Preview all dependent records and removed relationships before deleting a selection.
reader: steward
---

Deletion can remove more records than you selected. Read
[Changing many records](/docs/use/changing-many-records/) for permissions and
[Records](/docs/use/records/#deleting-a-record) for ownership rules.

1. Select the records in an editable collection. Check the workspace and whether you are in
   **Live** or a change set. Choose **Delete** in the selection bar.
2. Read the dialog and choose the offered refusal policy: delete those that can be deleted,
   or delete none unless all pass. Choose **Preview**.
3. Review selected records, children and owned dependants by type, relationships removed,
   and the connections reaching records that stay. A selected record may already be included
   beneath another selected record; do not count it twice.
4. Check the final total on **Delete … records**. This includes dependent records, not only
   selected rows. Use **Back** or cancel if the impact is wrong.
5. Confirm with **Delete … records**. Review the result and reasons for refusals. A new child
   or intervening edit can invalidate an item's preview. Use **Preview again** for refused or
   unprocessed items after reviewing their current impact.

Live removals take effect immediately. In a change set they remain proposals until publication.
History and earlier publications remain. A sampled preview cannot authorise deletion of items
it did not inspect. Cancellation stops remaining work and leaves already committed removals in place.

---
title: Follow a workspace
description: Receive publication notices for a workspace and stop them when no longer needed.
reader: steward
---

Follow a workspace to hear when someone else publishes a change set there. You need Browse
access. See [Notification settings](/docs/use/notifications-settings/#follow-publication-in-a-workspace).

1. Open **Home** and find the intended card under **Your workspaces**. Check the name and model
   before choosing **Follow**.
2. Check that the control reads **Following**. Your inbox now receives a notice for each
   eligible publication by another person. Your own publications do not notify you this way.
3. Open the notice from the bell or Notifications page. It opens the publication while that
   publication is active and you can browse it. Otherwise the notice becomes read and you
   remain in the inbox. A digest link behaves the same way.
4. For email copies, open **Settings › Notification email** and choose delivery for publication
   notices. Following itself works without mail.
5. To stop following, toggle **Following** on Home, or choose **Stop following** under
   **Workspaces I follow** in Notification email settings. Check the list after the change.

If you lose access or your account is deactivated, notices stop but the follow choice stays.
It can resume when access returns. If the toggle fails, read the error and retry rather than
assuming your preference changed.

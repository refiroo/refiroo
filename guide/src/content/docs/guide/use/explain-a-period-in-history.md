---
title: Explain a period in history
description: Ask for an account of changes, check its facts and open the underlying work.
reader: steward
---

Use this for a workspace's changes or one record's past. You need a configured AI provider and
**Browse** and **Use AI proposals** in the workspace. Counts and model labels go to the provider;
at values, names go too. Requests can incur costs and are recorded. Read
[AI and your data](/docs/use/ai-and-your-data/) and
[What changed in a period](/docs/use/history/#what-changed-in-a-period) for privacy and counting rules.

1. Open a record in **Live** and its **History**, or open **Settings › Workspaces**, choose the
   workspace and open **Activity**. Opening **What changed** sends nothing to AI.
2. In **Period**, keep **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days**, or choose **Choose dates**.
   Set **From** and **To, included**. Both dates count; the first must not be after the last.
   A workspace starts with seven days and a record with thirty.
3. Read the sending line, then choose **Explain this period**. **Writing the account** appears.
   A period without changes reports that nothing changed and nothing was sent to AI.
4. Read the statements and follow each numbered source to its fact. Expand **Show the other …**
   to inspect all the facts. The warning that AI can be wrong applies even when a source exists:
   check that the statement says what its numbers support.
5. Choose **Show it** or **Show them** beside a fact to narrow the current Activity or History.
   Use **Open the change set** or **Open the job** to inspect its preparation where offered.
   A missing job link can mean the job is no longer retained or you cannot open it.
6. Use **Show all activity** or **Show all history** to restore the full list. Change the period
   and ask again for a different interval, or use **Explain again** to refresh it. The same facts
   for exactly the same period within an hour reuse the earlier account without another provider
   call. Preset periods end at the time you ask, so **Explain again** can make a new paid call.

![A record's What changed section with date controls, statements and cited facts](/docs/screens/use-record-history-explained.png)

If **The account could not be written** appears, read the facts anyway and use **Try again**
when appropriate. If the request itself fails, its error also offers **Try again**. Neither
failure changes your records. For exact values and individual actions, use the normal
[History controls](/docs/guide/use/see-who-changed-what/); an account counts published changes,
not the record edits still waiting in an open change set.

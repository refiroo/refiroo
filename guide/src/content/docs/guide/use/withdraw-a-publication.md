---
title: Withdraw a publication
description: Remove the current designation while retaining the snapshot and Live data.
reader: steward
---

Withdraw when a publication should no longer be designated current. You need Publish in the
workspace. See [Publications](/docs/use/publications/) for what the designation means.

1. Open **Publications** and locate the row marked **Current**. Check its number and workspace.
2. Open the row's **More actions** and choose **Withdraw publication**.
3. Read **Withdraw Publication …?**. Confirm with **Withdraw publication**, or cancel to keep
   the current designation.
4. Check that the snapshot is **Withdrawn** and no publication is current. Live data, earlier
   publications and history remain. An earlier snapshot is not made current automatically.

If another publication became current meanwhile, the stale withdrawal can be refused. Reload
and review the current row before deciding again. This action does not retire the workspace,
delete the snapshot or undo its data changes. To prepare work from it, use
[Start from an earlier publication](/docs/guide/use/start-from-an-earlier-publication/).

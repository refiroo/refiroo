---
title: See who changed what
description: Trace an edit from a record, workspace activity or your own changes.
reader: steward
---

Choose the view that matches your question. See [History](/docs/use/history/) for the meaning
of authors, event kinds and publication context.

## Check one record

1. [Find the record](/docs/guide/use/find-records/) in the intended workspace and open it.
2. Scroll to **History**. Start with **All** and **By: Anyone** to avoid hiding an event.
3. Choose **Values**, **Relationships** or **Moves** to narrow the list, or select a person in **By**.
4. Read the action, author and time. Expand the entry's arrow to inspect the stored detail,
   such as the previous and new field values where recorded.
5. Check whether the action was prepared in a change set or applied by a publication before
   comparing it with the current fields.

![Record history below its relationships, with author and time on each event](/docs/screens/use-history-stop-changed.png)

*Above: Thusis Süd's history shows the correction and its later publication. The current field is THSD.*

## Check activity across records

1. Where available to your role, open **Settings**, **Workspaces** and select the workspace.
2. Choose **Activity**. It lists the newest actions first.
3. Under **What**, choose **Records**, **Relationships**, **Change sets** or **Publications**.
   Return to **Everything** to see their sequence together.
4. Read who acted and the context beside the time. Follow a linked person's name to inspect
   their changes if you need to trace their other work.

![Workspace activity with Everything selected](/docs/screens/use-history-activity.png)

*Above: Graubünden's activity connects the correction change set with the resulting publication.*

## Find your own work

1. Select the refiroo name to open Home. Read **Your recent changes** for recent actions.
2. Choose **See all your changes**. The **Your changes** page opens.
3. Choose **Workspace**, **What** and **Period**. Read each result's action and context.
   Widen those filters if the expected action is missing.

![Your changes with workspace, kind and period filters](/docs/screens/use-history-changes.png)

*Above: the list is narrowed to Guide Admin's work in Graubünden.*

A failed read needs a retry; an empty filtered result needs broader filters. If workspace
management is unavailable, use the record's History or Your changes, or ask someone with the
necessary access to inspect workspace Activity. History cannot restore a value by itself:
follow [Correct a record](/docs/guide/use/correct-a-record/) to make a new correction.

Under each day heading, read the newest entries first. On the record's History, use **Load older history**
to read 20 more after the initial ten and **Show fewer** to shorten the list. Check a job or
publication named as the cause; **client** marks a system's work, and **Before sign-in** marks an
older entry with no recorded author. Your changes has no value details or export: return to the
record's History for the saved values.

## Read an AI account or assistant origin

For a short explanation with links to the counted changes, follow
[Explain a period in history](/docs/guide/use/explain-a-period-in-history/). Opening History
sends nothing; asking for the account sends counts and, at values, names to the provider.
Calls can incur costs and are recorded; [AI and your data](/docs/use/ai-and-your-data/) explains
sending and retention.

On a record history entry, read **Proposed by the AI assistant** or **Prepared by an assistant**
alongside who asked, who edited and who published. These are different responsibilities.
An external assistant’s displayed name is its own claim. The personal Changes page has no
assistant filter; use [the record’s History](/docs/use/history/#work-prepared-by-an-assistant)
for the mark.

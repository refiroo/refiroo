Select the refiroo name in the top bar to return to **Home**. It brings together the workspaces
you can read and the work waiting for you. **Waiting for your approval**, where you may approve, and **Your change sets** appear above the workspace cards.

![Home with a greeting, workspace cards, open change sets and recent changes](/docs/screens/use-home.png)

*Above: Guide Admin’s Home shows two workspaces, Graubünden and Default, and one open change set. Your change sets lists work to continue; Your workspaces and Your recent changes follow.*

## Find a record

Use **Find a record in any workspace**, the box that reads **Find any record in any workspace (⌘K)**, to search record names across every workspace you may browse. Results are grouped by workspace and show the record's type and where it stands. Press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl+K elsewhere to focus the search, Up/Down to choose a result and Enter to open it. Escape closes the results. Check the workspace after opening a result.

## Your workspaces

The default workspace comes first. Each card shows the workspace name, its model and release, your roles there and its applications.
The **Default** badge identifies the default workspace. Choose an application button to open its
starting view in that workspace. Follow [Switch workspace](/docs/guide/use/switch-workspace/)
and [Open an application](/docs/guide/use/open-an-application/) to check the destination.

The card's counts lead to your change sets or the open change sets available to publish. A count
of open work does not say that it is validated or ready for publication.
**Nothing open here** means there are no open change sets counted on that card.

Choose **Follow** to receive a notice when someone else publishes a change set in that workspace.
Your own publications do not send you a followed-workspace notice.
The button changes to **Following**; choose it again to stop following. Following neither grants
access nor changes the records. Notices appear in your [inbox](/docs/use/inbox-and-approvals/).

## Continue where you left off

After you have worked in an application, a **Continue where you left off** card can name the last
place and workspace. Select it to return there. Check the banner on arrival before editing:
access and available data may have changed since your last visit.

![Home with a Continue where you left off card](/docs/screens/use-home-continue.png)

*Above: the card returns Guide Admin to Domains in Default, the last place visited.*

## Work waiting for you

**Your change sets** lists your open proposals, newest change first, with how much each changes. Open one to continue editing
or reviewing it. Follow [Prepare and publish a change set](/docs/guide/use/prepare-and-publish-a-change-set/)
for validation, review and publication.

Where you may approve requests, **Waiting for your approval** lists submitted change sets, newest
first. Each entry names the change set, workspace and submitter, and shows when it was submitted.
Choose **Review** to open its changes and decision controls. An open-change-set count on a workspace
card does not mean those change sets have been submitted for approval.

![Home showing a submitted change set under Waiting for your approval](/docs/screens/appr-home-waiting.png)

*Above: fictional Rita Approver is a Data owner in Graubünden. Guide approval example was submitted
by Guide Admin; Review opens the request.*

Follow [Approve a change set](/docs/guide/use/approve-a-change-set/) to review, send back or approve
and publish. A person required to obtain a second person's approval cannot decide their own
request. The [inbox](/docs/use/inbox-and-approvals/) also carries notices of waiting requests and
of the decisions on your submissions.

**Your recent changes** shows your latest actions. Consecutive steps on one model draft fold into one expandable line with their count; expand it to read each step. **See all your changes** opens the longer list,
with workspace, kind and period filters. See [History](/docs/use/history/) for what those entries mean.

The greeting names you and shows your roles. For the workspace-by-workspace detail, open your
[Account](/docs/use/account/). The avatar also opens theme choices and Sign out. The bell shows
unread notices and opens your [inbox](/docs/use/inbox-and-approvals/).

If Home reports a loading failure, retry it. If no workspace is available, ask an administrator
for Browse access to the workspace you need.

A saved address naming a workspace, application or record opens that place directly instead of
Home. If you may browse no workspace, Home says there is none yet beneath the instance's name;
it does not mean your sign-in failed. Ask an administrator for Browse access.

To manage following later, open **Settings › Notification email** and use **Stop following** beside
a workspace. Losing Browse access also stops its publication notices.

Following starts off. Losing Browse or deactivation pauses notices without deleting your
choice, so they can resume if access returns. **Stop following** deliberately removes the
choice. Follow [Follow a workspace](/docs/guide/use/follow-a-workspace/) for the steps and
[Notification settings](/docs/use/notifications-settings/) for email copies and digests.

A card can say **This version cannot open the workspace's model.** instead of offering
application buttons. Your data has not been deleted. Follow
[Open a retired or unavailable workspace](/docs/guide/use/open-a-retired-or-unavailable-workspace/)
to read the reason and find the responsible administrator.

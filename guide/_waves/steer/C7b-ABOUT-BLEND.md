# Steering for C7b (from the person who owns refiroo, 2026-11-03)

Add this to the problem refiroo solves, as a main part of the story on "What refiroo is" (and in a sentence on
the About page about bringing data in, if one exists; otherwise where loading data is mentioned):

Reference data in most organisations lives scattered across thousands of small spreadsheets, each team and
system with its own copy, its own columns and its own version of the truth. refiroo brings it into one place,
with a clear way of using it: the workbooks are loaded as they are (a workbook can even become the first
version of a model), checked against the same rules as every other change, and from then on there is one
agreed list with its history, its owners and its connections, which people edit in the browser, export
for colleagues as a workbook and load back, and which other systems read through the API.

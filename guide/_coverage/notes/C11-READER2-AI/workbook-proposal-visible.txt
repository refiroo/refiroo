Load a workbook
Haltestellen_Busbetrieb_Nord.xlsx

“Load this example public transport workbook into this empty workspace. Interpret stops, names and local codes and preserve UIC stop numbers as identifiers. Explain assumptions and omitted cells. Do not publish or activate anything.”

The workbook is ready to load, and the dry run refused nothing. I haven't loaded, installed, activated or published anything, and I touched no draft that isn't mine.

What gets loaded

Stops:
51 stops with their UIC number as the stop number. They sit under 13 municipalities taken from the names sheet. The workbook's Chur, Coira and Cuira all resolve to the one municipality Chur.
Names:
each municipality has a name per language (German, French, Italian, Romansh). Empty cells in that sheet simply have no name for that language.
Local codes:
the 25 Busbetrieb Nord codes are a new system with its codes under it, each linked to its stop. I added that system to the filter that decides which systems may map to stops.
Repeated columns as types of their own:
lines (1, 2, 4, 6) linked to stops, and platforms A, B and C as records under the stop they belong to. The tariff network, Tarifverbund Nord, holds zones Z1 to Z3, and each stop is linked to its zone.
Model changes in my draft:
Municipality no longer requires an FSO number and can stand without a district, because the workbook has neither.
Stops no longer require a translated name, because the workbook has none.
New fields on stop: accessible and remark.

Assumptions

I read J as accessible and N as not accessible.
The ?, n/a and - cells in lines, valid-from and barrier-free are treated as no value.
Platforms belong to a single stop, not shared across stops.
Dates are day.month.year.

Names kept as they are

Seven stops are spelled "Chur Albula" in the stop list and "Chur, Albula" in the code sheet. I kept the stop list spelling as the stop's name and stored the code sheet's spelling on the code. Two more stops lack the comma but have no code-sheet entry.
The remarks stay flat text rather than becoming a type.

Dry run:
213 new records and 164 new relationships. The only findings were 12 municipality names that are identical in several languages, which is expected.

I raised no questions, and no whole type moves, so the list of moves is empty.

The proposal is in proposal.json in this folder. (147 s, $0.55)
